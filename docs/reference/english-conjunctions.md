# English conjunctions and the relationships they signal

62 headwords, transcribed from Biblearc's *English conjunctions and their associated logical
relationships* sheet (`documents/Englishcongunctionsbracketingcheetsheetnewlogo.pdf`). A word with several
rows can signal any of them; the context decides. Scripture references are the sheet's own examples.

> **and** is the most ambiguous of the conjunctions and can stand for any relationship at all.

Bilateral (`BL`) is absent by design: it is Ground and Inference combined around one proposition.
