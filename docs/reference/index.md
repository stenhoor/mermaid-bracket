## Reading a bracket

A bracket joins two or more propositions and names the relationship between them.

- **Coordinate** relationships (`S` `P` `A`) join equals: two or more members under one label, no main point.
- **Subordinate** relationships join exactly two halves, each with its own label, one of which is the
  **main point** and carries a star.
- Colour follows the family: green coordinate, red support by distinct statement, blue support by
  restatement, orange support by contrary statement.

```mermaid
bracket
columns Text

Ac/Pur
  Ac   1
  *Pur If/Th
    Th   2
    If   3

1: And you, who once were alienated, he has now reconciled
2: in order to present you holy and blameless before him,
3: if indeed you continue in the faith.
```

The star sits on the arm that carries the argument: here the purpose, not the action.

## Abbreviations

| | | | | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `S` Series | `P` Progression | `A` Alternative | `G` Ground | `∴` Inference | `BL` Bilateral |
| `Ac/Res` Action-Result | `Ac/Pur` Action-Purpose | `If/Th` Conditional | `T` Temporal | `L` Locative | `Ac/Mn` Action-Manner |
| `Cf` Comparison | `-/+` Negative-Positive | `Id/Exp` Idea-Explanation | `Q/A` Question-Answer | `Csv` Concessive | `Sit/R` Situation-Response |
