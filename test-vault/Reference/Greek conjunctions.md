# Greek conjunctions and the relationships they signal

64 headwords, transcribed from Biblearc's *Greek conjunctions and their associated logical
relationships* sheet (`documents/Greekconjunctionsbracketingsheetsheetnewlogo.pdf`). As with the English
list it is an aid, not a rule: several entries carry more than one relationship and the context decides.

Bilateral (`BL`) is absent by design: it is Ground and Inference combined around one proposition.
